Project Overview

This research project examines the role of gamification elements and human factors in conversation games designed to support end-of-life decision-making.

The study adopts a mixed-methods approach to explore how people interact with these tools, with consideration of usability, technology acceptance, and the broader experience of engaging in potentially sensitive end-of-life conversations.

The research involves interviews and workshops and considers established evaluation frameworks, including the System Usability Scale (SUS) and Technology Acceptance Model (TAM).

NVivo is used for thematic analysis, while JASP supports descriptive and inferential statistical analyses.

This case study is currently in development. Further details regarding the research methodology, findings, and supporting visual materials will be added as the project progresses.
