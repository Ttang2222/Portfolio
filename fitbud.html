FitBud - Health & Fitness App Prototype

Figma · Teamwork · Prototyping · App Design

From: BET 350 - Customer Experience Design

Members: Aaron Liu, Hadeya Ikram, Navroze Antia, Tasmia Rahman, Tina Tang, Zain Jafrani

This project involved creating a health and fitness app with a virtual personal trainer of your choice. This virtual trainer helps engages with users through messaging and across the app, providing words of encouragement as a interactive yet easily accessible way for users totrack their workouts, nutrition, and overall well-being.
