FitBud - Health & Fitness App Prototype

A health and fitness app prototype featuring virtual personal trainers who support users through interactive messaging, encouragement, and fitness tracking.

FIGMA · TEAMWORK · PROTOTYPING · APP DESIGN
Course

BET 350 - Customer Experience Design

Team Members

Aaron Liu, Hadeya Ikram, Navroze Antia, Tasmia Rahman, Tina Tang, Zain Jafrani

Project Overview

This project involved creating a health and fitness app with a virtual personal trainer of your choice. This virtual trainer engages with users through messaging across the app, providing words of encouragement as an interactive yet easily accessible way for users to track their workouts, nutrition, and overall well-being.
