NeilMed: Journey Mapping

From: Personal Project 🧚

This project involved creating a journey map for the NeilMed product, visualizing the user's experience from initial contact to post-purchase support.

Section I: Journey Mapping

The journey map illustrates the user's interactions with the NeilMed product, highlighting key touchpoints, pain points, and opportunities for improvement. It provides a comprehensive overview of the user's experience, from initial awareness to post-purchase support.

Section II: Hierarchical Task Analysis (HTA)

Task Difficulty
1. Read Instructions Moderate
2. Finding the right time to use (eg, at least1hr before bedtime) Moderate
3. Water preparation (water volume, water temperature, water sterilization) of either distilled, micro-filtered (through 0.2 micron filter), reverse osmosis filtered, commercially bottled, previously boiled and cooled down water Difficult
4. Rinse bottle with prepared water Moderate
5. Fill bottle with water to line Difficult
6. Mix in saline packet Easy
7. Shake bottle with nozzle cap on Easy
8. Tissue preparation for in-between rinses Easy
9. Positioning head and body at an appropriate angle during use Difficult
10. Clean device with prepared water Difficult
11. Dry device accordingly with either a drying stand or a clean paper towel Moderate

    Section III: Methods & Instrumentation (Regulatory & Ergonomic Audit Protocol)

    This project involved conducting an audit of the NeilMed product, evaluating its compliance with standards and accessibility guidelines. To evaluate the usability and accessibility compliance of the NeilMed Sinus Rinse bottle, a mixed-methods audit protocol was established. Physical force requirements, dimensional accessibility, visual typography, and safety labeling were measured against recognized human factors and regulatory benchmarks: ADA/AODA, ISO 9241-11 / ISO 9241-210, SOR/2019-244, and EN 16186.
