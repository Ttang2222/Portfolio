SYSTEMS MAPPING

Affinity Diagramming · Prototyping · Usability Testing · A/B Testing · Heuristic Evaluation · User Research

Systems mapping is a visual tool used to understand the relationships and interactions within a complex system. It helps identify key components, their connections, and the flow of information or resources.

Affinity Diagramming

Affinity diagramming is a method used to organize and categorize ideas, observations, or data into groups based on their natural relationships. It is often used in brainstorming sessions, user research, and design thinking processes to identify patterns and insights from qualitative data.
