Course

SYDE 644 - Human Factors Testing

Team Members

Amit Bains, Kshin Patel, Sneha Shah, Tina Tang

After noting down real-time observations and creating affinity diagrams to identify gaps in the existing solution of mounting brackets onto a wall with a regular drill, my group and I listed the associated design requirements and created a screwholder prototype to accompany the drill in the same bracket-mounting task. For the report, I was in charge of the statistical analysis to test the hypotheses between conditions A(without screwholder) and B(with screwholder) on behalf of the team.
